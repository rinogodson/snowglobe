## 🔮 SnowGlobe | A Gift to your loved ones! 
Stack: ReactJS, TailwindCSS

### Create digital SnowGlobes and gift them to your friends this Christmas!
#### This README was made using [READMEwizard](https://readme-wizard-three.vercel.app) (it's an app made by me :) [Check it out!](https://github.com/rinogodson/readme-wizard))

## Usage:
Just click the Blue "Flip SnowGlobe" button to flip the snowglobe, or if you're on your phone, just try rotating your phone!

You can create your own SnowGlobe by just clicking the red '{+}' icon and entering your or your friends. 
Name, and click the copy button to copy the link to your clipboard, and then share it.

## Extra Features:
- You can use the light bulb button for a more amazing aesthetic experience.
- You can use the snowflake button to change the no. of snowflakes.
